EVALUATION FRAMEWORK // 04

Comparison Criteria & Audit Methodology

Our editorial team maintains active contractor accounts across each network to audit workflow stability, billing correctness, and legal mechanics under Nordic tax regulations.

01 // Disbursement Reliability

Zero-tolerance tracking for retroactive wage reductions, uncompensated review queues, or platform payout delays beyond scheduled dates.

02 // Assessment Transparency

Verifying that platforms disclose real project queue health before requiring applicants to complete extensive unpaid onboarding tests.

03 // Nordic Tax & B2B Legality

Invoicing validation under Finnish (Toiminimi/Oy) and Swedish (Enskild firma/AB) reverse-charge VAT frameworks (EU Directive 2006/112/EC).

04 // Queue Stability (Zero-Task Ratio)

Monitoring the prevalence of the 'empty queue' state—measuring the realistic monthly hours an active contractor can reliably bill.
