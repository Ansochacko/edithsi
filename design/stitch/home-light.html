05 // FIELD QUESTIONS HONEST FAQ

Unvarnished clarification on compensation mechanisms, statutory taxation, and real intake probabilities.

Direct operational answers

No hype, no recruitment promises. Read before submitting your credentials.

01 Is it free to apply?
expand_more
Yes, completely free. There are no registration fees, certifications to purchase, platform access charges, or upfront equipment payments. Any service asking you to pay to join an AI contractor talent pool is illegitimate.
02 Will I definitely get work?
expand_more
No. Passing the verification interview qualifies you for active talent pools, but actual project dispatch depends entirely on AI research lab demand cycles, your language pairs, and specific project specifications. Having a verified profile is a prerequisite, not a guarantee of continuous hourly allocation.
03 How am I paid?
expand_more
Contractors receive payments directly via standard international wire transfer or contractor platforms like Deel. Settlements are issued in USD or converted directly to EUR on bi-weekly or monthly cadence according to verified project timesheets.
04 What about taxes in Finland and Europe?
expand_more
You operate as an independent contractor or sole trader (toiminimi in Finland). Income is treated as foreign earned income or business revenue; you are legally obliged to report these earnings to the Finnish Tax Administration (Verohallinto) or your national fiscal office. You are responsible for local pension (YEL) and tax prepayments.
