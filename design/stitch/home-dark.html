05 // FIELD QUESTIONS HONEST FAQ

Unvarnished answers on European cross-border tax, platform dynamics, and work security.

Statutory and operational inquiries

01 Is it free to apply?
expand_more

Yes, completely free. There are never any fees, certifications to purchase, training courses to complete, or upfront costs. Anyone requesting money on behalf of Mercor or Edithsi is fraudulent.

02 Will I definitely get work?
expand_more

No. Passing the interview qualifies you for project batches, but work volume depends entirely on AI lab demand cycles. Some pools receive immediate weekly allocations, while others remain inactive for months. Treat this strictly as supplementary, variable freelance capacity.

03 How am I paid?
expand_more

Disbursements occur via direct bank transfer (IBAN/SEPA) or Deel in USD or auto-converted EUR, paid bi-weekly or monthly per the specific client engagement terms. Time tracking is handled within the platform workspace.

04 What about taxes in Finland and Europe?
expand_more

You operate as an independent contractor (B2B or individual sole proprietor / toiminimi). You must declare foreign earnings to Vero (Finnish Tax Administration) or your respective local tax office. You are liable for your own social security contributions (YEL in Finland) and income taxation.
