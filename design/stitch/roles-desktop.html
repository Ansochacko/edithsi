Operational prerequisites for Nordic & European specialists

01. 100% Free Intake

Applying is entirely free. Never pay third parties claiming to guarantee Mercor contracts or selling assessment answers.

02. Contractor Status

You operate as an independent contractor (B2B/1099 equivalent). You supply your own workstation, hardware, and statutory coverage (YEL in Finland).

03. Fiscal Responsibility

Gross payouts are not pre-taxed. Reporting is mandatory via your tax administration (Vero, Skatteverket). Cross-border EU invoices apply standard reverse-charge mechanics.

04. Verification Security

Official invites and evaluation communications arrive strictly from verified domains ending in @mercor.com.
