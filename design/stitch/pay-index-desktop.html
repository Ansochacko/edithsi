05.4 // METHODOLOGICAL PROTOCOL

Methodology & Sampling Integrity

The Edithsi AI Work Pay Index is curated as an empirical audit rather than a self-reported impressionistic poll. Because AI evaluation work is governed by rigid Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) that deter candid compensation discussions, wage disparity across different recruiting funnels has historically remained opaque.

To construct this registry, data points are collected through three strictly gated channels:

  1. Redacted Compensation Contracts: Direct submission of initial engagement agreements, statement of work (SOW) documents, and formal platform offer sheets, audited by our research desk.
  2. Disbursement Slips: Regular bi-weekly payment verification via Stripe Express, Deel, Wise, and Hyperwallet remittance summaries.
  3. Institutional Sourcing Requisitions: Direct inspection of enterprise client rate tables requested by foundation model labs specifically targeting the Nordic-Baltic knowledge pool.

Rates fluctuate dynamically in response to foundation model training cycles. When frontier labs reach the terminal pre-release evaluation phase for high-stakes capability sets (e.g. doctoral biology, legal discovery synthesis, distributed systems architecture), emergency surge premiums of up to 40% are frequently introduced for 6–10 week sprint intervals before returning to baseline medians.

Statutory Tax & Withholding Guidance (Finland / Nordics) VERO FI REF-91

All rates exhibited throughout this registry reflect gross contractor billables (US Dollar / Euro equivalents) prior to local value-added taxes, municipal income taxes, or national declarations. Platforms such as Mercor, micro1, Outlier, and Alignerr classify European contributors as independent contractor entities (itsenäinen ammatinharjoittaja / toiminimi).

  • Finland (Vero): Gross proceeds must be reported under business income or overseas work income. Contributors earning above the annual statutory threshold (€9,010.28 in 2024/2026) are legally obligated to maintain independent Entrepreneur Pension Insurance (Yrittäjän eläkevakuutus / YEL).
  • Sweden (Skatteverket): Income falls under F-skatt self-employment declarations.
  • Value Added Tax (VAT): Export of professional evaluation services to United States corporations typically qualifies as reverse-charge VAT outside EU territory, subject to formal tax account review.
