## Step 1: How Weekly USD Stripe Payouts & EUR Bookkeeping Work

On Mercor, active contractors are usually paid **weekly via Stripe in US dollars (`USD`)**. When Stripe transfers your payout to your Finnish bank account (SEPA IBAN), your gross contract earnings in USD are converted into euros (`EUR`).

For clean Finnish bookkeeping, keep these practical records from day one:

1. **Save Weekly Stripe Payout Statements:** Every weekly transfer generates a breakdown showing the gross USD amount approved on the platform, any currency conversion rate applied, and the exact net EUR amount deposited into your Finnish bank account.
2. **Record Revenue and Expenses in Euros (`EUR`):** Finnish tax declarations and accounting ledgers are maintained strictly in euros. Whether you record the actual EUR amount settled into your bank account or convert USD invoices using the European Central Bank (ECB) reference rate on the transaction date alongside FX differences, stay consistent across the tax year.
3. **Track Deductible Home-Office and Equipment Costs:** As a self-employed contractor in Finland, legitimate expenses incurred to earn income—such as a dedicated USB microphone for the AI interview, workspace deductions (*työhuonevähennys*), or accounting software—can generally be deducted against your self-employment revenue according to `vero.fi` guidelines.

### Once You Are Matched to an Active Project:
- **Prepayment Tax (*Ennakkovero*) in OmaVero:** Because no employer withholds tax from your Stripe payouts, you estimate your annual self-employment profit in the Finnish Tax Administration's online portal (*OmaVero*) and apply for **prepayment tax (*ennakkovero*)**. OmaVero generates bank reference slips so you can pay income tax in monthly or bi-monthly installments rather than facing a large back-tax bill (*jäännösvero*) plus interest the following year.
- **When a *Toiminimi* Makes Sense:** If your AI-training contract work becomes recurring, registering a *toiminimi* via `suomi.fi` / the Finnish Patent and Registration Office (*PRH*) gives you a Business ID (*Y-tunnus*) and a structured framework for single-entry bookkeeping (*yhdenkertainen kirjanpito*). Furthermore, because you are supplying B2B services to a company established outside the EU (in the United States), you should check `vero.fi` rules on international reverse-charge VAT registration and thresholds.

## Step 3: The 2026 YEL Pension Insurance Threshold (`EUR 9,423/year`)

In Finland, pension insurance for self-employed individuals is governed by the Self-Employed Persons' Pensions Act (*Yrittäjän eläkelaki* — **YEL**), overseen by the Finnish Centre for Pensions (*Eläketurvakeskus*, `etk.fi`) and provided by pension insurance companies such as Ilmarinen, Varma, Elo, or Veritas.

Here is the key figure you must know for **2026**:

- **2026 YEL Minimum Work Income Threshold:** **`EUR 9,423 per year`** (roughly `EUR 785 per month`).
- **When YEL Becomes Mandatory:** If you are aged 18–68, your self-employed activity continues for at least four consecutive months, and your estimated annual **YEL work income (*YEL-työtulo*)**—the reasonable monetary value of your work input—is at least **`EUR 9,423/year` (2026)**, you are legally required to take out YEL insurance within six months of starting that level of work.
- **Why Variable AI-Training Hours Require Careful YEL Monitoring:** Because Mercor project hours are never guaranteed, a contractor working on a high-rate track like **Software Engineering Expert** (`$70–150/h` posted range on `2026-11-05`, not guaranteed) or **Medical Expert** (`$60–180/h` posted range on `2026-11-05`, not guaranteed) can cross `EUR 9,423` in gross billings during a busy multi-month project, whereas someone logging occasional hours on a short sprint might stay below the threshold or below the four-month continuity rule. Keep note that YEL contributions are fully tax-deductible in Finland.

## Critical Warning If You Receive Finnish Unemployment Benefits

If you receive earnings-related unemployment allowance (*ansiopäiväraha* from an unemployment fund / *työttömyyskassa*) or basic unemployment benefits from Kela, read this rule carefully:

> **If you are on unemployment benefit, you must report self-employment to the employment authorities before you start working.**

In Finland, employment authorities assess whether your entrepreneurial activity is full-time (*päätoiminen yrittäjyys*) or part-time (*sivutoiminen yrittäjyys*). Even a small amount of unreported independent contractor work can trigger benefit clawbacks.
