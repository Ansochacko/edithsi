## What Mercor Actually Is (And What It Is Not)

Mercor is a talent marketplace and contractor platform connecting subject-matter experts—lawyers, physicians, software engineers, finance specialists, linguists, and generalist writers—with AI labs that need human experts to write complex prompts, evaluate reasoning chains, verify code, and perform reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF).

Before applying, understand three core boundaries:

1. **Independent contractor status, not employment:** If matched to a project from Finland or elsewhere in Europe, you work as a self-employed independent contractor. Mercor does not withhold national taxes, pay employer social contributions, or provide paid holiday accrual.
2. **A talent-pool funnel, not an immediate job seat:** Public listings on `work.mercor.com` primarily feed a searchable candidate pool. Your résumé and automated AI interview are indexed so project managers can filter for your credentials when a client funds a matching dataset batch.
3. **100% free to apply:** Applying is free; never pay anyone for a job, assessment review, or onboarding slot. If anyone asks for payment, read my guide on [how to spot fake AI job scams](/how-to-spot-fake-ai-job-scams/).

## How the Talent Pool & Project Matching Model Works

Most frustration among European applicants comes from confusing *completing the interview* with *receiving an active contract*. Here is how the process works in practice:

### 2. Waiting Weeks (Or Getting No Project Offer)
After you finish the assessment, your profile enters the talent pool. **Work is not guaranteed.** Many qualified people wait weeks before hearing back, and many receive no project offer at all. Project matching depends entirely on shifting client demand—one month labs may need Finnish and French audio evaluators, while the next month demand shifts to licensed lawyers or Python engineers.

### 3. Project Onboarding and Variable Hours
If your profile matches an active project, you receive an email invitation outlining the project's hourly rate, weekly hour cap, and onboarding steps. Even on an active project, work volume can pause or conclude once the client's dataset target is completed.

## How Weekly USD Payouts via Stripe Work in Europe

When you log approved hours on an active project, you are paid as an independent contractor, usually weekly via **Stripe** in **US dollars (USD)**.

For contractors in Finland and across Europe, keep three practical factors in mind:

- **Stripe Payouts & Currency Conversion:** You link your European bank account (SEPA IBAN) through Stripe. Because Mercor pays in USD, your final EUR or local-currency deposit depends on the EUR/USD exchange rate and Stripe's conversion spread at payout time.
- **Taxes and YEL Pension Insurance:** You are responsible for all national tax obligations. In Finland, for example, you must handle prepayment tax (*ennakkovero*), decide whether a sole proprietorship (*toiminimi*) is appropriate, and enroll in YEL pension insurance once your estimated annual work income reaches `EUR 9,423/year` (2026 threshold). This is not tax advice.
- **Unemployment Benefits:** If you receive unemployment benefits, you must report self-employment to the employment authorities **before** you start working.
