### 1. Lead with Your Highest Verifiable Credential
If you hold a clinical credential (doctor, nurse, dentist, pharmacist, public health specialist), a law degree, a software engineering background, or an audit/finance qualification, prioritize that specialist pool first. Frontier AI labs need domain specialists to write prompts, evaluate model outputs, and verify reasoning in complex technical scenarios where generalist reviewers cannot spot subtle errors.
