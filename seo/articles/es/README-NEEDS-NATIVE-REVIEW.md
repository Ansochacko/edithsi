# Why Spanish (`es`) Long-Form Articles Require Human Native Review

All planned Spanish (`es`) profession hubs and spokes listed in `/seo/content-plan.csv` are gated with `reviewStatus: "NEEDS NATIVE REVIEW"` and `draft: true` until reviewed by a native Spanish speaker.

## Why Machine Translations Are Blocked
1. **Agencia Tributaria (AEAT) & RETA Precision:** Independent contractors in Spain billing a US entity (`work.mercor.com`) in USD via Stripe face specific obligations under *Agencia Tributaria* (*alta en el Censo de Empresarios Modelo 036/037*, non-EU B2B VAT rules, and *IRPF Modelo 130*) and *Seguridad Social* (*RETA cotización por ingresos reales*), as well as *SEPE* unemployment compatibility rules. Machine translations must never be published without native verification of these terms.
2. **Spanish LSSI-CE Ad Disclosure Compliance:** Under *Ley 34/2002 (LSSI-CE)*, referral links (`?referralCode=02e0c12a-d634-4738-9686-04f28f31f52b`) must be unambiguously labeled as advertising (`PUBLICIDAD` / `enlace de referido · publicidad`).
3. **Linguistic Quality for Spanish Evaluators:** Because Mercor's `Language & Audio Expert ($35–50/h)` role explicitly recruits Spanish language specialists, our Spanish hubs and spokes must be reviewed by a native linguist before publication.
